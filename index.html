Welcome to the BLOW THINGS UP LAB

WTF is the BTU?

The ATLAS BTU Lab is an inclusive community makerspace facilitating projects at the intersection of Art, Design, Engineering and Architecture. It serves multiple functions as a general workspace, and workshop host. Any CU student can gain access to BTU. Our goal is to provide the most diverse, inclusive environment for makers of all types in a highly experimental and productive space.
